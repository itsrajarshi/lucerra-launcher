Privacy and cookies

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and has no cookie banner, because there is nothing to consent to. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and has no forms or accounts. It stores nothing in your browser.

What loads

The fonts, images and film are served from this same site. Nothing is fetched from third-party servers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The app

Lucerra Launcher declares no network permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It collects nothing.

Feedback

The feedback form runs in your browser and sends nothing to this site. When you press Open on GitHub it takes you to GitHub with your text pre-filled, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies from there.

Links

The GitHub, LinkedIn and donation (UPI) links leave this site. Those services have their own policies.
